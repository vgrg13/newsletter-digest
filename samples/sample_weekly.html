⬡  Week in Review

This week's newsletters told a coherent story about an economy navigating the late stages of a rate cycle while a technology revolution reshapes capital allocation. The AI infrastructure arms race (GPT-5, Blackwell Ultra, Mistral Large 2) is accelerating faster than enterprise absorption capacity, creating a paradox where capability improves but pricing power softens. The Fed's hold-and-cut signal and cooling CPI provide the macro permission structure for the VC rebound, but the IPO gap means the ecosystem's liquidity plumbing is still impaired. Watch two things next week: hyperscaler capex guidance in earnings calls (the real AI infrastructure signal) and any follow-through on the Commerce Department's new export control draft.

◆Claude's Analysis

The AI infrastructure theme is entering a phase where hardware efficiency gains and model architecture improvements (mixture-of-experts, sparse activation) are outrunning the ability of enterprise buyers to absorb them. This creates a paradox: pricing power for frontier APIs weakens even as capex requirements for training remain enormous. Watch for hyperscaler capex guidance revisions in earnings calls — AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud will signal whether they're leaning into or pulling back from the infrastructure build-out.
