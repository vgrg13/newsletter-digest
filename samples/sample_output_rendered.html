⬡  Today's Big Picture

Today's dominant thread is the interplay between AI capability escalation and its economic consequences — GPT-5's launch, NVIDIA's Blackwell Ultra, and the open-source efficiency push from Mistral are all moves in the same game, each compressing the cost or capability frontier from a different angle. The Fed's hold-and-cut signal provides the macro backdrop that keeps risk appetite alive for the VC rebound Axios documents, though the IPO gap is a real constraint on ecosystem health. The SaaS bifurcation Clouded Judgment surfaces is the quiet story worth watching: AI-native multiples expanding while legacy NRR declines is an early signal of where the market thinks the transition is heading.

The Fed's hold-with-cuts-ahead posture is a Goldilocks outcome for risk assets — it removes near-term tightening risk while confirming the disinflation story is intact. The key watch shifts to labour data: if payrolls stay above 150k and wage growth cools below 4%, the June cut becomes consensus.
